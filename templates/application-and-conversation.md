## Review rules

- Changed recipient, text, attachment selection, or conversation context requires fresh review.
- A queue entry does not prove contact started; contact started does not prove a resume was sent.
- A draft does not prove delivery. Do not automatically retry an unconfirmed attempt.
- A recognized WeChat string does not prove identity or completed addition.
- A closed conversation stays closed unless the candidate deliberately resumes it.
